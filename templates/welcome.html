About OralVision

OralVision is a cutting-edge platform developed at GM University that uses artificial intelligence to detect potential oral lesions and early signs of oral cancer from images.

Our mission is to improve early detection rates of oral cancer, which significantly increases survival rates and treatment success. By making screening accessible and convenient, we aim to save lives through early intervention.
